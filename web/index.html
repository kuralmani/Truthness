Truthness Open source. Runs on your machine, with the model you choose.

Fact-check a video or article

Truthness pulls out the claims, checks each one against published sources, and reports what holds up, what doesn't, and what is being sold.

How it works

  1. Reads the contentCaptions for videos, the article text for pages.
  2. Checks each claimThe model you choose looks for evidence and links the sources it used.
  3. Scores the whole thing0 is entirely false, 100 is accurate. "Misleading" means real facts used to create a false impression.

A verdict here is an AI's assessment, not a ruling. Always open the sources.
